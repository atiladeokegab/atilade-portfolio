# atilade.uk

**Platform:** web, Next.js on Vercel. **Users:** recruiters, founders, judges, IBM Z and hackathon
people, and agents reading `/llms.txt` and the markdown routes. **Purpose:** show who Atilade Oke is
and what Atilade builds, with proof, and make Atilade easy to reach.

**Positioning:** an engineer and founder who ships. Founder of Refinery (AI change-attestation for
enterprise COBOL), Analyst, Data & AI at ION, Manchester EEE graduate, eight hackathon wins.

**Brand commitments:** white page, Inter, near-black ink with grey words for the connective tissue of
a sentence (the hero's signature), Instrument Serif for rare display moments, real photos only, no
stock. Plain first-person copy, specific and sourced. The lead's taste: Linear, Stripe, Apple.

**Evidence:** only facts already on the site or on the public LinkedIn CV. Nothing invented.

**Principles:** say it plainly; show the real thing; one memorable moment per page; restrained motion
that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; works at 390 and 1280.
