# Refinery x IBM Bob

The average COBOL programmer is over 55, and the systems they maintain move trillions a day. IBM Bob can read the code and suggest changes, but the reasons behind it, the threshold hardcoded after an incident in 1987, live in one person's head.

Refinery captures them. Every flag, every rejection, every "this is wrong because" is recorded and fed back into Bob's knowledge base.
