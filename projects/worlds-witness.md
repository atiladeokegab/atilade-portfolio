# Witness, Worlds London

London's first World Models hackathon. We won it.

Witness statements are hard to picture. We turned one into a world.

The witness looks around the real street on a phone. The interviewer takes a still, draws boxes where the witness says things were and labels them: "white van, kerbside, facing away". Each box is inpainted and handed to a live world model as its reference, so you can walk around the scene.

When the witness corrects something, only that box is regenerated. The rest of the street stays put.

## Stack

Next.js, React, Runware, Reactor LingBot World, phone motion sensors.
