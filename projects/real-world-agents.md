# Scale, Real-World Agents Hackathon

Hosted by Dwelly in London. Banking and Financial Services track.

A suggestion is not permission. Scale reads a customer case the way a bank's back office gets it, the AI proposes one next step, and plain code checks it before anything runs.

## Results

- 9 of 10 on our judgement tests: identity removed, hidden instructions, contradicted amounts, distress, role violations, ambiguous references
- Every case comes with the agent's reasoning written down

## Stack

Python, LLM agents, our own eval harness with injected faults.
