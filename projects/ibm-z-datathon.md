# IBM Z Datathon 2026, Manchester

This time I was on the other side of the table.

IBM chose the University of Manchester as the only UK host for its global Datathon, which drew over 20,000 registrations from more than 60 countries. Our IBM Z Society committee planned it from the summer: co-chairs Diana Zhu and the rest of the team, with me alongside them.

## Results

- **400+ registrations** in Manchester, three times our original goal
- Feedback called it one of the biggest events the IBM Z Student Ambassador Program has run
- A LinkedIn feed full of participants sharing what they built

## Thanks

Joe Winchester, Ben Davis and Lewis J. at IBM, Lella Violet Halloum, Lucas Sahn, Vaidehi Suthar and Nithin Kurian, the Shooting Stars Foundation, Your Big Year, and the Department of Computer Science at the University of Manchester.
