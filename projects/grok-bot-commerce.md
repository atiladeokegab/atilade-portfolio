# HyperDrop, Grok Bot Commerce London

Built in one day at Fleek with Jack and Kaan. Track: a merchant that runs itself.

Launching a clothing brand takes weeks and four different skills. HyperDrop does it from one sentence: idea, market research, the decisions it can't make itself, six designs, a live Shopify store with print-on-demand fulfilment, a launch post and a sales assistant with a discount code.

Afterwards we kept going: a few weeks of fixes, better research and a dev version the team can open.

## Stack

Grok (xAI), Shopify, print-on-demand, Higgsfield.
