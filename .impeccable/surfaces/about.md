# Surface brief: /about

**Scope and mode:** one page, Read mode with a Persuade first screen. An established world: the home
page's type, colours and photos. No new tokens.

**Audience:** someone who just met Atilade (a recruiter, a founder, a judge) and wants the person
behind the projects in under a minute.

**Job:** who Atilade is, what Atilade builds now, how Atilade got here, the proof, the communities, how to reach Atilade.

**Action:** send an email, or open LinkedIn or GitHub.

**Proof:** the dated story, the wins with what each was, the IBM Z Society numbers.

**Constraints:** facts only from the site and the public CV. Labels at least 12px, focus rings,
no horizontal scroll at 390, reduced motion respected. No kicker labels above headings.

## Direction contract

- **One feeling:** "I know this person now", calm confidence.
- **One memorable thing:** the story as years. Each year is a huge Instrument Serif numeral that
  sticks while its chapter scrolls past (CSS `position: sticky`, no script), so the page reads like
  turning the pages of three years.
- **Leaves out:** the 3D lanyard (heavy WebGL, a stock React Bits piece, and it said "play with me"
  rather than "this is me"), the skills tag cloud (LinkedIn holds it), stat-card grids.
- **Hard limit:** one serif moment per screen, everything else Inter.
